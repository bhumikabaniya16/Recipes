Pasta Fazool (Pasta e Fagioli)

Ingredients

  • Small Pasta (like Ditalini or Macaroni): 1 cup
  • Cannellini Beans or Kidney Beans: 1 can (rinsed and drained)
  • Olive Oil: 2 tablespoons
  • Onion, Carrot, and Celery: 1 cup total (finely diced - traditional soffritto)
  • Garlic: 2 cloves (minced)
  • Crushed Tomatoes: 1 cup
  • Vegetable or Chicken Broth: 4 cups
  • Italian Seasoning: 1 teaspoon
  • Parmesan Cheese: For serving
  • Salt and Black Pepper: To taste

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté the Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion, carrot, and celery, and sauté for 5 minutes until soft. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Simmer the Broth: Pour in the crushed tomatoes, vegetable broth, and Italian seasoning. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
  3. Add Pasta and Beans: Stir in the pasta and drained beans. Reduce heat and simmer for 8-10 minutes until the pasta is tender and cooked through.
  4. Season and Serve: Season with salt and pepper to taste. Ladle into bowls, top with freshly grated Parmesan cheese, and serve warm!

Lasagna Flate bread

Ingredients

  • Lasagna Noodles: 9-12 sheets
  • Ground Beef or Italian Sausage: 500g
  • Marinara or Pasta Sauce: 3 cups
  • Ricotta Cheese: 2 cups
  • Mozzarella Cheese: 2 cups (shredded)
  • Parmesan Cheese: ½ cup (grated)
  • Garlic and Onion: 1 each (minced/chopped)
  • Salt, Pepper, and Italian Seasoning: To taste

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil the Noodles: Cook lasagna noodles in a large pot of boiling salted water according to package instructions until al dente, then drain.
  2. Make the Meat Sauce: Sauté the onion and garlic in a pan, add the ground beef and cook until browned. Stir in the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Assemble the Layers: In a baking dish, layer a bit of meat sauce, lasagna noodles, ricotta cheese, and mozzarella. Repeat the layers until the dish is full, topping with extra mozzarella.
  4. Bake: Cover with foil and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20 minutes, then remove foil and bake for another 10 minutes until bubbly and golden brown. Let cool before slicing!

Spicy Tuna Pasta with Creamy Fra Diavolo Sauce

Description

A bold and fiery Italian-inspired pasta dish featuring flaky tuna tossed in a rich, creamy, and spicy tomato Fra Diavolo sauce with garlic and red chili flakes.

Ingredients

  • Pasta (like Penne or Spaghetti): 250g
  • Canned Tuna in Olive Oil: 1-2 cans (drained)
  • Crushed Tomatoes: 1 cup
  • Heavy Cream: ½ cup
  • Garlic: 3 cloves (minced)
  • Red Chili Flakes (or Calabrian Chili): 1-2 teaspoons (adjust for heat)
  • Olive Oil: 2 tablespoons
  • Fresh Basil or Parsley: Chopped for garnish
  • Salt and Black Pepper: To taste

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil the Pasta: Cook your choice of pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Make the Spicy Base: Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and red chili flakes, sautéing for about 1 minute until fragrant and golden.
  3. Build the Sauce: Pour in the crushed tomatoes and let simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in the heavy cream and let the sauce thicken slightly into a rich, creamy pink hue.
  4. Add Tuna and Pasta: Gently fold in the flaked tuna and cooked pasta. If the sauce is too thick, splash in a little of the reserved pasta water. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Garnish and Serve: Top with fresh chopped parsley or basil and serve hot!
