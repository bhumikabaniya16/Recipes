Gajar Halwa

Recipes in Steps

Ingrediants

  • Carrots: 500g (preferably red, juicy carrots)
  • Milk: 2 cups (full-fat / whole milk)
  • Ghee: 3 to 4 tablespoons (clarified butter)
  • Sugar: ½ cup (adjust to taste)
  • Cardamom Powder: ½ teaspoon
  • • Nuts: 2 tablespoons of chopped almonds, cashews, and pistachios
👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Prepare the Carrots : Wash, peel, and finely grate the carrots
  2. Saute the Carrots:
    1. Heat 1 tablespoon of ghee in a heavy-bottomed pan or kadhai.
    2. Add the grated carrots and sauté on medium heat for 5 to 7 minutes until they change color slightly and the raw smell disappears.
  3. Simmer in Milk
    1. Pour in the milk and stir well
    2. Bring it to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low. Cook until the milk completely evaporates, stirring frequently to prevent it from sticking to the bottom.
  4. Add Sugar and Ghee:
    1. Once the milk is absorbed, add the sugar and the remaining ghee.
    2. The sugar will melt and turn liquid. Keep stirring and cooking until the moisture completely evaporates and the halwa thickens.
  5. Garnish and Serve:
    1. Garnish and Serve:
    2. Cook for another 2 minutes until the halwa starts leaving the sides of the pan. Serve hot!

Your Halwa is ready enjoy it with your family

Panner Butter Masala

Ingredients

  • Paneer: 250g (cut into cubes)
  • Butter: 3 tablespoons
  • Onion: 1 medium (finely chopped or pasted)
  • Tomato Puree: 1.5 cups
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste: 1 tablespoon
  • Cashews: 10-12 (soaked and blended into a paste)
  • Fresh Cream: 3 tablespoons
  • Kasuri Methi (Dried Fenugreek Leaves): 1 teaspoon
  • Spices: Red chili powder, garam masala, and salt to taste

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the Base:
    1. Heat 1 tablespoon of butter in a pan and lightly fry the paneer cubes until golden, then soak them in warm water to keep them soft.
    2. In the same pan, add a bit more butter, sauté the ginger-garlic paste and onions until golden brown.
  2. Make the Tomato Gravy:
    1. Add the tomato puree and cook until the oil starts separating from the mixture.
    2. Stir in the cashew paste and red chili powder, cooking for another 3 minutes.
  3. Simmer and Add Cream:
    1. Add a cup of water to adjust the gravy consistency, followed by salt and garam masala. Let it simmer.
    2. Add the fried paneer cubes and crushed kasuri methi into the gravy.
  4. Final Touch:
    1. Turn off the heat, stir in the fresh cream and remaining butter.
    2. Serve hot with naan, paratha, or jeera rice!

Your delicious Paneer Butter Masala is ready! Enjoy your meal.

Aaloo parantha

Ingredients

  • Whole Wheat Flour (Atta): 2 cups
  • Boiled Potatoes: 3 medium (mashed)
  • Green Chilies: 1-2 (finely chopped)
  • Coriander Powder: 1 teaspoon
  • Garam Masala: ½ teaspoon
  • Amchur (Dry Mango Powder): ½ teaspoon
  • Salt and Red Chili Powder: To taste
  • Ghee or Butter: For roasting

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Make the Dough: Mix whole wheat flour with water and salt to knead a soft, smooth dough. Let it rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Prepare the Filling: In a bowl, mix mashed potatoes, green chilies, coriander powder, garam masala, amchur, red chili powder, and salt.
  3. Stuff the Dough: Take a ball of dough, flatten it slightly, place a portion of the potato filling in the center, and seal the edges together.
  4. Roll and Cook: Gently roll the stuffed ball into a flat circle. Place it on a hot tawa, roast with ghee or butter on both sides until golden brown spots appear, and serve hot!

Dal Chawal

Ingredients

  • Yellow Toor Dal (Pigeon Peas): 1 cup
  • Basmati Rice: 1 cup
  • Ghee or Oil: 2 tablespoons
  • Cumin Seeds (Jeera): 1 teaspoon
  • Garlic: 3 cloves (chopped)
  • Turmeric Powder and Salt: As needed
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves: For garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the Rice: Rinse basmati rice and boil it in water until fluffy and tender. Set aside.
  2. Cook the Dal: Wash toor dal, add turmeric, salt, and water, and pressure cook for 3-4 whistles until completely soft. Whisk it smooth.
  3. Make the Tadka: Heat ghee in a small pan, add cumin seeds and chopped garlic until golden and fragrant.
  4. Combine: Pour the hot tempering directly into the cooked dal. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve piping hot over the cooked rice.

Kachori

Ingredients

  • All-Purpose Flour (Maida): 2 cups
  • Urad Dal or Moong Dal: ½ cup (soaked and coarsely ground)
  • Fennel Seeds (Saunf) and Coriander Seeds: 1 tablespoon (crushed)
  • Oil or Ghee: For deep frying and kneading
  • Spices: Red chili powder, dry ginger powder, garam masala, and salt

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the Dough: Mix flour, a pinch of salt, and a little warm oil, then knead into a firm dough using water. Let it rest.
  2. Make the Filling: Sauté the coarsely ground soaked dal in a pan with crushed fennel seeds, coriander seeds, red chili powder, and salt until dry and aromatic.
  3. Assemble: Divide the dough and filling into small equal balls. Stuff a filling ball inside a flattened dough piece and seal the edges tightly.
  4. Deep Fry: Flatten gently with your palms, then drop into medium-hot oil and fry on low-medium heat until golden brown and crispy. Serve warm!
