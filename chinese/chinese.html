Authentic Chinese Egg Rolls

Ingredients

  • Egg Roll Wrappers: 8-10 sheets
  • Ground Pork: 150g (cooked and seasoned)
  • Green Cabbage: 2 cups (finely shredded)
  • Carrot: ½ cup (julienned)
  • Bean Sprouts: ½ cup
  • Garlic and Ginger: 1 teaspoon each (minced)
  • Soy Sauce: 1 tablespoon
  • Sesame Oil: 1 teaspoon
  • Cooking Oil: For deep frying
  • Slurry: 1 tablespoon flour mixed with a little water (to seal the wrappers)

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the Filling:
    1. Heat a pan with a little oil, sauté the minced garlic and ginger until fragrant.
    2. Add the ground pork, shredded cabbage, carrots, and bean sprouts. Stir-fry on high heat until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
    3. Season with soy sauce and sesame oil, then let the mixture cool completely.
  2. Wrap the Egg Rolls:
    1. Lay an egg roll wrapper flat like a diamond shape on your counter.
    2. Place a spoonful of the cooled filling near the bottom corner. Fold the bottom corner over the filling, fold in the left and right sides, and roll it up tightly.
    3. Seal the final corner using the flour-and-water slurry.
  3. Deep Fry and Serve:
    1. Heat cooking oil in a deep frying pan over medium heat.
    2. Carefully drop the wrapped egg rolls into the hot oil and fry until they turn golden brown and crispy (about 4-5 minutes).
    3. Drain excess oil on paper towels and serve hot with sweet and sour sauce!

Your crispy, authentic Chinese egg rolls are ready to enjoy!

Wonton Soup

Ingredients

  • Wonton Wrappers: 20-25 sheets
  • Ground Pork: 150g
  • Green Onion: 2 stalks (finely chopped)
  • Ginger: 1 teaspoon (minced)
  • Soy Sauce: 1 teaspoon
  • Sesame Oil: 1 teaspoon
  • Chicken Broth: 4 cups
  • Baby Bok Choy: 2-3 heads (halved)
  • Salt and White Pepper: To taste

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Make the Wonton Filling: Mix the ground pork, half of the chopped green onions, minced ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and white pepper in a bowl.
  2. Wrap the Wontons: Place a small spoonful of filling in the center of each wrapper, wet the edges with water, and fold it into your favorite wonton shape.
  3. Boil the Wontons: Drop the wontons into a pot of boiling water for 3-4 minutes until they float to the top, then remove and set aside.
  4. Prepare the Soup: Bring the chicken broth to a simmer in a pot, add the bok choy until tender, then add the cooked wontons. Garnish with remaining green onions and serve hot!

Green Onion cake

Ingredients

  • All-Purpose Flour: 2 cups
  • Boiling Water: ¾ cup
  • Cold Water: ¼ cup
  • Green Onions (Scallions): 1 bunch (finely chopped)
  • Sesame Oil: 2 tablespoons
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon
  • Cooking Oil: For pan-frying

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Make the Dough: Pour boiling water into the flour while stirring, then add cold water. Knead into a smooth dough, cover, and let rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll and Layer: Divide dough into equal balls. Roll each ball into a thin flat circle, brush with sesame oil, sprinkle with salt and a generous amount of green onions.
  3. Form the Coil: Roll the flat circle tightly into a cylinder shape, then coil it into a snail-like round. Roll it out flat again gently.
  4. Pan-Fry: Heat a pan with a little oil over medium heat. Cook the pancake for 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown and crispy. Serve hot with dipping sauce!
