Donggeurangttaeng (Korean Meat Patties)

Ingredients

  • Ground Pork: 200g (or a mix of pork and beef)
  • Firm Tofu: 100g (mashed and excess moisture squeezed out)
  • Green Onion: 2 tablespoons (finely chopped)
  • Garlic: 1 clove (minced)
  • Soy Sauce: 1 teaspoon
  • Sesame Oil: 1 teaspoon
  • All-Purpose Flour: ¼ cup (for coating)
  • Eggs: 2 (beaten for the egg wash)
  • Salt and Black Pepper: To taste
  • Cooking Oil: For pan-frying

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the Mixture:
    1. In a large bowl, combine the ground pork and mashed tofu.
    2. Add the chopped green onion, minced garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and black pepper. Mix thoroughly until well combined.
  2. Shape the Patties:
    1. Take small portions of the meat mixture and roll them into small balls, then gently flatten them into round, bite-sized patties.
  3. Coat for Frying:
    1. Lightly dust each patty in all-purpose flour, shaking off any excess.
    2. Dip the floured patties into the beaten eggs so they are fully coated.
  4. Pan-Fry and Serve:
    1. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium-low heat. Place the patties in the pan.
    2. Fry on both sides until golden brown and cooked through (about 3-4 minutes per side). Serve warm!

Your delicious Korean Meat Patties are ready! Enjoy your meal.

Bok Choy Kimchi

Ingredients

  • Baby Bok Choy: 4-5 heads (washed and halved or quartered lengthwise)
  • Coarse Salt: 1 tablespoon (for quick salting)
  • Korean Red Pepper Flakes (Gochugaru): 2 tablespoons
  • Fish Sauce: 1 tablespoon (or soy sauce for a vegetarian version)
  • Minced Garlic: 1 teaspoon
  • Minced Ginger: ½ teaspoon
  • Sugar: 1 teaspoon
  • Green Onion: 2 stalks (chopped)
  • Toasted Sesame Seeds: 1 teaspoon

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep and Salt the Bok Choy:
    1. Wash the bok choy thoroughly and cut them into halves or quarters.
    2. Sprinkle coarse salt lightly over the leaves and let them sit for 15-20 minutes to soften slightly, then rinse gently with cold water and drain well.
  2. Make the Kimchi Paste:
    1. In a mixing bowl, combine the Gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes), fish sauce, minced garlic, minced ginger, and sugar. Mix well into a thick paste.
  3. Toss and Serve:
    1. Add the drained bok choy and chopped green onions into the bowl with the seasoning paste.
    2. Gently toss everything together using your hands (wear kitchen gloves!) until the bok choy is evenly coated. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and serve fresh!

Your crunchy, fresh Bok Choy Kimchi is ready to enjoy!

Tadbooki

Ingredients

  • Korean Rice Cakes (Tteokbokki-tteok): 300g (soaked in warm water for 10 minutes if frozen or hard)
  • Korean Fish Cakes (Odeng): 2-3 sheets (cut into bite-sized pieces)
  • Water or Broth (Anchovy or Kelp broth): 2 cups
  • Korean Red Chili Paste (Gochujang): 2 tablespoons
  • Korean Red Chili Flakes (Gochugaru): 1 tablespoon (adjust for spice)
  • Soy Sauce: 1 tablespoon
  • Sugar: 1 to 2 tablespoons
  • Garlic: 2 cloves (minced)
  • Green Onions: 2 stalks (cut into 2-inch pieces)
  • Toasted Sesame Seeds: For garnish

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Make the Sauce Base:
    1. In a wide pan or skillet, add 2 cups of water or broth.
    2. Stir in the Gochujang (chili paste), Gochugaru (chili flakes), soy sauce, sugar, and minced garlic. Mix well and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  2. Cook the Rice Cakes:
    1. Once the sauce is bubbling and slightly thickened, add the drained rice cakes and fish cakes into the pan.
    2. Stir gently to prevent the rice cakes from sticking to the bottom.
  3. Simmer and Thicken:
    1. Reduce the heat to medium-low and let it simmer for about 8 to 10 minutes, stirring frequently until the rice cakes become soft and chewy and the sauce reduces into a rich, glossy glaze.
  4. Garnish and Serve:
    1. Add the green onions in the last minute of cooking, give it a quick mix, and turn off the heat.
    2. Transfer to a serving bowl, sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds, and enjoy hot!

Your delicious Tteokbokki is ready! Enjoy your meal.
